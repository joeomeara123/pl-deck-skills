02 — The Architecture

The Agentic Organisation

Three layers form the Agentic Governance Framework. They sit between raw enterprise chaos and the action layer where agents and humans create value. Without them, nothing works.

03 — Bottleneck A: Data Pipeline

Data sits in silos, managed by humans.

Teams copy-paste between CRM, support tickets, emails, Slack, and Excel spreadsheets with no version control. This works when things move slowly; it breaks the moment feedback loops accelerate.

Siloed and Manual
Data lives across dozens of apps, stitched together by people copy-pasting between tabs.
Spreadsheet Hell
Critical business logic in Excel. No version control. Prone to human error. Breaks at speed.
04 — Bottleneck B: Permissions and Security

What agent and human can access what data?

It is not just about what agents can see. It is about defining the blast radius of every action, per function and per agent. GDPR and SOC2 become hard constraints.

Role-based access Blast radius limits Rollback triggers Audit trail GDPR SOC2
"If your agents can see everything, your risk surface is everything."
05 — Bottleneck C: Process Layer

Processes are not known. They live in people's heads.

Every function (sales, support, growth, product) runs on processes that are tribal, undocumented, and inconsistent. Nobody knows where the real bottlenecks are, so nobody knows where AI can actually create value.

Discover and Map
Surface what processes actually exist (the real ones, not the documented ones) and codify them.
Prioritise
Not every process should be automated. Identify high-value targets per function.
06 — The Bottleneck Thesis

Point solutions will fail without the underlying infrastructure.

When feedback loops accelerate, copy-pasting between spreadsheets breaks.

Action Layer: collapsing
Near-zero cost to build. Foundation model companies move extremely fast and innovate at unprecedented speed. Building AI tools is no longer the hard part.
Governance Framework: the bottleneck
Data, permissions, and process layers are where enterprises get stuck. Without these, no amount of agentic tooling will translate into real value.
07 — Go-To-Market

Start with the process map. Everything else follows.

1
Deploy the workflow mapper
An agent connects to Slack, email, CRM, support tools and observes actual patterns. Who sends what to whom. What triggers what. Where things get stuck. Builds a process graph in 2 to 4 weeks.
2
Map reveals the data gaps
The process map exposes where data lives in silos, what is manually copy-pasted, and where there is no version control. This is the business case for the data pipeline.
3
Define permissions and controls
Now you know what data exists and who touches it. You can define what agents and humans should access, set blast radius limits, and wire up compliance.
4
Ship agents on the foundation
With data unified, permissions set, and processes mapped, deploy agentic automations on the highest-value workflows first. Each one is backed by real data and governed from day one.
