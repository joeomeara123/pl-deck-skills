The thesis

PROGRESSION LABS
Why art, why now

Blue-chip art behaves unlike the markets you already own.

Across the past two decades, top-tier art has shown low correlation to equities and bonds. The best works are scarce by definition, and that scarcity compounds as an artist's cultural standing grows.

How we select

PROGRESSION LABS
Methodology

Five signals we weight

01

Institutional validation

Museum acquisitions, biennial selections and placement in respected private collections.

02

Auction momentum

Rising sell-through rates, repeat bidding and a steady multi-year price trend.

03

Gallery backing

Representation by a blue-chip primary gallery that actively manages supply.

04

Scarcity and provenance

Limited output, a clean ownership history and strong physical condition.

05

Cultural relevance

Critical attention and alignment with a generational shift in taste.

PROGRESSION LABS
Featured, emerging

Spotlight: Jade Fadojutimi

Born in 1993, Fadojutimi became one of the youngest artists ever acquired by the Tate. Her large, high-key abstractions pair museum validation with primary representation from Gagosian.

Auction results have climbed quickly since 2021, with several works clearing seven figures against far lower estimates.

Institutional demand plus constrained supply is the clearest setup for durable appreciation.

The risk is real. Early-market prices can move sharply in both directions, so position sizing matters more here than anywhere else in the portfolio.
